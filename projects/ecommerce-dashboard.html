E-commerce Website Traffic & Sales Analysis

SQL • Python • Power BI • Power Query • DAX

Designed an end-to-end business intelligence solution to analyze website traffic, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, conversion funnel and sales trends. The project combines SQL, Python, Power Query and Power BI to transform raw transactional data into an interactive executive dashboard that supports data-driven business decisions.

Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate millions of website sessions, thousands of customer transactions and multiple marketing campaigns. Without proper analytics it becomes difficult to understand which marketing channels drive sales, which products perform best, where customers abandon the purchase journey and how business performance changes over time.

Project Overview

This project transforms raw website traffic, order, customer and product datasets into an interactive Power BI dashboard consisting of three analytical pages. Users can explore website performance, sales trends, customer behavior and product-level insights through dynamic filtering, drill-through navigation and KPI tracking.

Dashboard Walkthrough

The dashboard contains three analytical pages designed for different business users. Each page focuses on a different stage of the customer journey—from website acquisition to product performance and overall sales.

Website Traffic Analysis

Analyze website sessions, visitor acquisition, marketing campaign performance, conversion funnel, hourly traffic patterns and customer conversion rates.

Product Analysis

Evaluate product revenue, purchase conversion, monthly orders, product returns and customer buying behavior across different products.

Sales Analysis

Monitor total revenue, monthly sales, MoM order growth, product contribution, profitability and business performance across multiple years.

Dashboard Features

Interactive KPI Dashboard

Executive KPI cards provide instant visibility into revenue, orders, customers, conversion rate, website sessions and profit.

Advanced DAX Measures

Developed custom DAX measures for conversion rates, profit calculations, MoM growth, customer retention and revenue KPIs.

Drill-through Analysis

Users can navigate from summary sales dashboards directly into detailed product performance pages.

Cross Filtering

Selecting any chart dynamically filters every related visualization for faster exploratory analysis.

Technical Highlights

Data Preparation

Cleaned and transformed multiple datasets using Power Query and SQL before building the analytical model.

Data Modeling

Built a relational data model connecting orders, customers, products and website sessions for accurate business reporting.

DAX Measures

Created custom KPIs including Revenue, Profit, Conversion Rate, Month-over-Month Growth, Funnel Conversion and Product Contribution.

Interactive Reporting

Implemented slicers, drill-through pages, cross-filtering and dynamic visualizations for interactive business analysis.

Business Impact

This dashboard enables business stakeholders to monitor website performance, evaluate product profitability, understand customer purchasing behavior and identify marketing campaigns generating the highest return. By integrating sales, traffic and customer data into one interactive dashboard, decision-makers can quickly identify revenue opportunities, optimize marketing spend and improve conversion performance.
