LEGO Analytics Dashboard

Power BI • DAX • Power Query • Excel

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze over three decades of LEGO product releases, pricing trends, themes, category performance, and product evolution. The solution enables business users to explore historical trends through interactive filtering, cross-highlighting, and detailed analysis.

Business Problem

LEGO has launched thousands of products across different themes, age groups and price ranges over multiple decades. Understanding historical release patterns, theme popularity and pricing strategies helps identify business trends and supports future product planning.

Project Overview

This project transforms raw LEGO product data into an interactive Power BI dashboard where users can explore releases, compare themes, analyze pricing trends, evaluate product categories and identify long-term business insights through dynamic visuals.

Dashboard Walkthrough

The project contains three dashboard pages designed for different analytical needs. Users can move across pages to explore executive KPIs, individual LEGO sets and detailed theme performance.

Executive Dashboard

Provides a complete business overview including KPIs, yearly release trends, average pricing, category distribution and theme performance.

Set Explorer

Explore every LEGO set with interactive filters including release year, theme, age category, price and product details.

Theme Analysis

Compare LEGO themes based on release volume, pricing patterns, product mix and contribution across multiple decades.

Dashboard Features

Cross Page Navigation

Cross Filtering

Key Insights

Theme Performance

Identified the highest-performing LEGO themes based on product releases and overall portfolio contribution, helping highlight the brand's strongest product lines.

Pricing Trends

Analyzed average product pricing across more than three decades to identify premium segments and long-term pricing evolution.

Product Evolution

Explored how LEGO expanded its product portfolio over time by comparing yearly releases and category growth.

Category Analysis

Compared products across age groups and themes to understand portfolio distribution and release frequency.

The final solution delivers an interactive business intelligence dashboard that enables users to analyze over 4,300 LEGO products through dynamic filtering, cross-page navigation and visual exploration. The dashboard simplifies historical trend analysis, theme comparison and pricing evaluation while demonstrating strong skills in Power BI, DAX, data modeling and business storytelling.
