Customer Shopping Behaviour Analytics

Python • Power BI • DAX • Customer Analytics • Business Intelligence

Customer behaviour is one of the strongest indicators of business performance. In this project, I analyzed 4,000 customer transactions from an e-commerce retail dataset to understand purchasing patterns, customer demographics, subscription behaviour, product category performance, and customer satisfaction. Python was used for exploratory data analysis and data preparation, while Power BI was used to build an interactive dashboard that enables business users to explore customer insights through dynamic filtering and KPI-driven storytelling.

Project Overview

Retail companies collect thousands of customer transactions every day, but raw transactional data rarely provides meaningful business insights. The objective of this project was to transform customer purchasing data into an interactive analytical dashboard capable of answering key business questions related to customer demographics, subscription adoption, shopping behaviour, product performance, payment preferences, and customer satisfaction. Using Python and Power BI, the project converts thousands of records into actionable insights that support marketing decisions, customer retention strategies, and revenue optimization.

Business Questions

  • Which age groups contribute the highest sales?
  • How are customers distributed by gender?
  • What percentage of customers are subscribed?
  • Which payment methods are preferred?
  • Which shipping methods are most frequently selected?

  • What is the average customer review rating?
  • How many previous purchases does an average customer make?

Key Insights

Revenue Drivers

Clothing emerged as the highest revenue-generating product category, indicating strong customer demand and consistent purchasing behaviour across apparel products.

Subscription Opportunity

More than 70% of customers were non-subscribers, revealing a significant opportunity to increase recurring revenue through membership programs and loyalty campaigns.

Customer Demographics

Young adult customers represented the largest purchasing segment, making them the primary target audience for personalized marketing initiatives.

Customer Satisfaction

Average customer review ratings remained consistently positive, suggesting healthy customer satisfaction while highlighting opportunities to improve lower-rated products.

Purchase Behaviour

Purchase frequency and previous buying history revealed that returning customers contribute a significant portion of overall sales.

Interactive Analytics

Business users can dynamically explore customer behaviour using filters for gender, season, category, subscription status, payment method, and shipping type.
